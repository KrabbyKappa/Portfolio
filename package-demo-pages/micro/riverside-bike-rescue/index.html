Roadside triage menu

Clear repair boundaries make the call faster.

Flat rescue

Usually roadside: tube replacement, tubeless plug attempt, tire boot, and pressure check.

May need shop if tire casing is torn.

Chain reset

Usually roadside: dropped chain, stiff link check, quick drivetrain reset, and safe get-home test.

May need shop if hanger or cassette is damaged.

Brake rub

Usually roadside: wheel seating, pad alignment, cable tension check, and ride-safe adjustment.

May need shop if rotor or rim is bent.

Commuter check

Usually roadside: lights, bolts, tire pressure, and obvious hazards before rolling again.

May need shop for full overhaul.
