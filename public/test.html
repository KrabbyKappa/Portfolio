Our Story

From Tropical Farms to Your Table Since 2010

BananaCo was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to the freshest, most nutritious bananas on the planet. We partner directly with family-owned farms across Ecuador, Costa Rica, and the Philippines.

Every bunch is hand-selected at peak ripeness, carefully packed in eco-friendly materials, and delivered within 48 hours of harvest. No wax coatings, no artificial ripening — just pure, natural goodness.
